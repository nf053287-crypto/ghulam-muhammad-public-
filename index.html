Our Story

Our History

Founded over 15 years ago by Dr. Mushtaq Ahmed Paracha & Dr. Habib Jehan, Ghulam Muhammad Public School was established with a vision to provide quality, affordable education to every child, regardless of their financial background. The school operates under Anjuman Ta'awn Foundation, a non-profit, NGO-based organization dedicated to serving the community through education and social welfare.

Since its inception, Ghulam Muhammad Public School has remained committed to creating a nurturing learning environment where students are encouraged to achieve academic excellence while developing strong moral values, confidence, leadership, and a sense of social responsibility.

Our dedicated and experienced teachers combine modern teaching methods with character-building principles to ensure the holistic development of every student. Along with academic success, we emphasize discipline, respect, compassion and lifelong learning, preparing students to become responsible citizens and future leaders.

Over the past 15 years, the school has earned the trust of parents and the community by providing high-quality education in a caring and inclusive environment. Many of our students have gone on to excel in higher education and various professional fields, reflecting our commitment to educational excellence.

As a proud initiative of Anjuman Ta'awn Foundation, Ghulam Muhammad Public School continues to uphold its mission of making quality education accessible to all. We remain dedicated to empowering future generations with knowledge, integrity and the skills needed to make a positive impact on society.
