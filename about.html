About Us

Committed to Excellence in Education and Character Building

Our Vision

To build an educated, compassionate and empowered society by providing every child, regardless of financial background, with access to quality education, moral values and equal opportunities. As an NGO-based institution, we envision nurturing confident, responsible and lifelong learners who will contribute positively to their communities and the nation.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality, affordable and inclusive education to every child through a safe, supportive and value-based learning environment. As a non-profit initiative of Anjuman Ta'awn Foundation, we are committed to empowering underprivileged children with knowledge, character, life skills and confidence, enabling them to achieve their full potential and become responsible citizens of society.

Founder - Late Dr. Mushtaq Ahmed Paracha

Dr. Mushtaq Ahmed Paracha is the Founder of Ghulam Muhammad Public School. With a vision to provide quality education to the community, he started the school in 2011 with only 10 students and classes up to the primary level. Through dedication, hard work and commitment to educational excellence, the school has grown remarkably over the years. Today, Ghulam Muhammad Public School proudly provides education up to the matric level with more than 300 students. His vision and continuous efforts remain the foundation of the school’s progress and success.

OUR FACULTY

Dedicated & Highly Qualified Faculty

One of the most essential pillars of any educational institution is its teachers. At Ghulam Muhammad Public School, our faculty members are dedicated professionals who inspire students through knowledge, integrity and exemplary character. We believe that education is not only about academic excellence but also about nurturing values such as honesty, responsibility, confidence and compassion.
