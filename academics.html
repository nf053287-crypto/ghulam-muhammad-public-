Official Affiliation

Our Partnership with Jugnuu

Ghulam Muhammad Public School proudly collaborates with Jugnuu Organization to empower students through education, digital literacy, leadership development and career opportunities.

Jugnuu is a youth empowerment organization committed to preparing students for a successful future through practical learning and skill development. The organization provides training in Digital Skills, Communication Skills and Financial Literacy, enabling students to become confident, independent and career-ready. Jugnu also identifies students with exceptional potential and connects them with internships, employment opportunities and professional development programs, helping them build successful careers and make a positive impact in their communities.
