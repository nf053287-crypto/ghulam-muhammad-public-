Admission Steps

1. Application Form

Complete the online application form available on our website. Ensure all required fields are filled.

2. Document Submission

Submit the necessary documents including previous academic records and birth certificate.

3. Interview

After review, you will be invited for an interview with the admissions committee.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the age requirement for admission?

Children must be at least 4 years old by September 1st of the application year for kindergarten.

Are there any entrance exams?

Yes, basic evaluation tests for English, Urdu, and Mathematics are conducted for primary classes.
